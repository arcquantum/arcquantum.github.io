Research Interests & Objectives

I pursue research in theoretical and computational Quantum Physics, specializing in Quantum Information, Quantum Many-Body Physics, and Tensor Networks. My goal is to develop mathematical and computational frameworks that bridge quantum information with quantum field theory, relativity, and the foundations of quantum mechanics. I am deeply interested in rigorously solving fundamental problems in physics, with a particular focus on understanding entanglement dynamics, open-system many-body phenomena, and quantum metrology in complex quantum systems.
